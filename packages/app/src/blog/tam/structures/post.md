# TA Structures

Optional algebraic and geometric enrichments of Trajectory-Affordance.

These structures are not required for the core TA definitions or TA-Learn dynamics. They provide one interpretation that enables additional reasoning.

## Affordance Lattices

Treats affordance cones as elements of a complete Heyting algebra. Enables logical operations (meet, join, implication) on cones.

## Port Manifold

Equips port space with smooth manifold structure and a Riemannian metric. Enables continuous modes of interaction and geometric notions of port similarity.

## Port-Affordance Fibration

Bridges port geometry and affordance logic via a Grothendieck fibration. Requires both structures above. Shows how geometric moves in port space lift to affordance refinements.
